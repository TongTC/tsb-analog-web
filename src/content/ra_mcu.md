<p style="text-align:left">The Renesas RA (Renesas Advanced) Family is a modern line of 32-bit microcontrollers (MCUs) built on ARM® Cortex®-M cores. Unlike Renesas' proprietary RX or RL78 families, the RA series allows developers to leverage the massive global Arm ecosystem while benefiting from Renesas’ specialized peripheral IPs and security features.
<p style="text-align:left">
Think of the RA family as Renesas’ "open" platform—designed for everything from tiny battery-powered sensors to high-end AI at the edge.
</p>
